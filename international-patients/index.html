Your questions

Before you make plans

Can you confirm treatment before I arrive?

We can discuss your concern and appointment planning. Final recommendations, costs and treatment schedules require an appropriate clinical examination and any necessary investigations.

Can all treatment be completed in one visit to India?

This depends on the treatment, clinical findings, laboratory work and follow-up needs. Please discuss your full travel window with the clinic; completion within a particular stay cannot be assumed.

Who provides implant, surgical and orthodontic treatment?

Visiting dentists provide these treatments by arrangement. The clinic must confirm the appropriate dentist’s availability and the review schedule for your case.

Can I share my existing X-rays or treatment plan?

Contact the clinic first to arrange an appropriate way to share your records privately. Please do not enter clinical records in the public enquiry form.

Where is the clinic?
