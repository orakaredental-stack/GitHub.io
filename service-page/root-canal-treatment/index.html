Root canal treatment in Dehradun

Orakare Dental Clinic offers root canal treatment for teeth affected by deep decay or infection. An examination helps determine whether this is an appropriate way to retain your tooth.

Book an appointmentCall the clinic

What is root canal treatment?

Root canal treatment removes infected pulp from inside a tooth. The canals are cleaned, filled and sealed. A crown may be needed afterwards.

Arrange an assessment

If you have tooth pain or concerns about a damaged tooth, contact the clinic. Your dentist will explain the findings, suitable options and costs before you decide how to proceed.

What to expect

Local anaesthetic is usually used. Treatment can require more than one appointment; the plan depends on the tooth and your individual needs. Follow-up appointments are part of completing your care.

Common questions

Will I need a crown?

A crown may be recommended. Your dentist will discuss the restoration needed for your tooth.

How much does treatment cost?

Ask the clinic for an estimate after assessment, including any recommended restoration and follow-up visits.
